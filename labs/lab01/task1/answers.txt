No, reordering the gate instantiations in Task 1(b) does not change the
final logical result because Verilog gate instantiations are concurrent
structural statements; their order in the source file does not determine
the order in which the gates execute.

In Task 1(c), adding delays changes the timing of the waveform and makes
propagation delays visible. The final logical values of sum and cout
remain the same after the circuit settles, but transitions occur later
because signals must propagate through the delayed gates.

1(b): No meaningful waveform/timing change due solely to gate reordering.
1(c): Yes, the waveform timing changes because of the added gate delays.
Final settled logic remains identical.
